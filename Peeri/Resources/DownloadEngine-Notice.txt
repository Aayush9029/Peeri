Peeri bundles aria2 1.37.0, libssh2 1.11.1, and OpenSSL 3.6.4.

aria2 is Copyright 2006–2019 Tatsuhiro Tsujikawa, licensed under GPL-2.0-or-later. Its bundled SFTP support links libssh2 and OpenSSL statically. HTTPS uses the macOS TLS implementation. The accompanying aria2, libssh2, and OpenSSL license files contain their notices and terms.

The complete source archives and build instructions for the bundled engine are available in Peeri-1.1-engine-sources.tar.gz alongside the v1.1 release:
https://github.com/Aayush9029/Peeri/releases/tag/v1.1

https://github.com/aria2/aria2
https://libssh2.org/
https://openssl-library.org/

Peeri also bundles FFmpeg and FFprobe 9.0.1, Copyright the FFmpeg developers, licensed under LGPL-2.1-or-later. These helpers merge downloaded streams without transcoding and are built without external libraries. The included FFmpeg license and the release source archive provide the terms and build instructions.
